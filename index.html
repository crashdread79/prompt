01 / The essentials

What makes a great prompt?

A prompt is what you type or share with ChatGPT to tell it what you need. You don’t need magic words — just enough direction.

01

State the task

Start with a clear action: explain, summarize, brainstorm, compare, write or plan.

02

Add useful context

Say who it’s for, what you already know and any relevant background or examples.

03

Specify the output

Ask for a checklist, table, steps, email, word limit or a particular tone.

A simple formula to remember

Task + Context + Audience + Format + Constraints

Use the parts that actually matter — not every prompt needs all five.

04 / Level up
Six habits of better prompting
Simple techniques you can use right away.
1
Say what success looks like
Explain how you will judge a useful answer, not only what topic you want.
2
Show an example
Provide a sample of the style, structure or result you prefer.
3
Set a clear scope
Give a word count, timeframe, reading level or budget if relevant.
4
Refine your first answer
Ask ChatGPT to shorten, clarify, expand or revise what it just produced.
5
Check important facts
Verify factual claims, references, calculations and critical advice independently.
6
Protect private information
Remove passwords, account numbers, personal identifiers and confidential material.
